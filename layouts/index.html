For a year and a half I was one of five engineers building Shazam — the app that let you point a phone at a speaker and find out what was playing. The recognition engine was a black box to me. My work was everything around it: making something probabilistic and invisible feel instant and trustworthy to people who had never heard the word fingerprint.

That turns out to be good preparation for now, when a lot of companies have a model and no real idea how to put a product around it.
