I have been shipping software for over twenty years, and mobile for most of the time the iPhone has existed. Usually solo or in a small team, and often walking into something that is already on fire.

The short version of the CV: one of five engineers on the iOS team at **Shazam**; **Lead iOS Developer at Learn With Homer**, where I trained the whole engineering team on Swift and led them through the company's acquisition; **sole iOS developer at River Health**, a telehealth platform, for three years; and **CTO at Wingman**, where I took the entire product back from the offshore agency that had built and controlled it.

I work through my own UK limited company and have done since 2014, for US and UK clients. For a long stretch of that I was represented by [10x Management](https://10xmanagement.com), who still put work my way on a non-exclusive basis.

## The other half

I am also a composer and pianist, recording as **Larkhall**. I studied at the University of Cambridge's Centre for Music and Science and was previously a prizewinning concert pianist. I am currently building **Play & See**, an interactive installation that lets anyone explore a piano connected to a live visuals system, funded by Arts Council England, Bath Spa University and the Marchus Trust.

That is not a hobby I keep separate from the engineering. It is real-time graphics, sensor input and live systems that have to survive unsupervised public use — and the underlying problem is the one I have been working on the whole time: making a complex system feel obvious to someone who has never seen it before.

There is more of that, going back to 1997, at [c.harl.ie](https://c.harl.ie).
